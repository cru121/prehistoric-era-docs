Secret Society — Fire & Stone

A Prehistoric-exclusive Secret Society. Requires the Secret Societies game mode.

Spirits of Fire and Stone

Discovered at Tribal Villages (70% chance)

The Spirits of Fire and Stone are an animist guild of artisan-shamans who monopolize the mystery of transformation. They focus on Production, Resources, and Military.

There was a rite. It did not take long. Now you see it. It was they who chose the stone of your walls. The flagstones under your chair, your father's sword, all of it passed through their hands. The master mason greets you this morning as he has every morning. It is the same greeting as yesterday, and now you know what it means. The servants no longer carry kindling. The hearths of the palace do not go out at night, and no one finds this strange. You lay a hand on the wall. It is winter. The stone is warm. You are now of the Fire and Stone.

Titles

As you earn Governor Titles and advance through the eras, you unlock these tiers — each granting new powers (the Athanor building, Emberwright unit, and the Star-Quickening project).

TitleUnlocked byEffect
The EmberBy discovering a Tribal Village

Completing a Mine or Quarry on a resource turns that resource into a Baetyl and replaces the improvement with a Baetyl Crater. Baetyl Craters give a major adjacency bonus to Industrial Zones. Baetyls are a luxury resource, and each yields 1 of every unlocked mineral strategic resource per turn.

The CrucibleIn the Medieval Era

Allows you to construct the Athanor building, a powerful replacement of the Workshop. An Athanor grants an Emberwright when completed.

The FurnaceIn the Industrial Era

Emberwrights may be purchased with ✨ Faith. When an Emberwright Reforges, the target unit's tile and adjacent valid land tiles turn into Volcanic Soil. Industrial Zones gain +1 🔨 Production for each adjacent Volcanic Soil.

The Star-ForgeIn the Atomic Era

Unlocks the Star-Quickening, a city project that provides ✨ Faith each turn and GreatEngineer Great Engineer points on completion. On completion it also grants one Star-Seed, which an Emberwright may plant to create a Baetyl Crater.
