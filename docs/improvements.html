Tile Improvements

12 improvements built during the stone age — foraging camps, pit houses, palisades, cairns and ritual sites.

⬦

Hunting Target

—

A hunting target belonging to your civilization. Move any outbound hunting party onto this tile and use Hunt Large Game. On success, carry the carcass to any city you own and unload it.

⬦

Trade Foraging

—

A Foraging Band gathers a resource's surplus for a one-time yield, without consuming the resource. What it yields depends on the resource: raw materials such as Stone, Copper, and Ivory give 🔨 Production; foodstuffs and trade goods such as Wheat, Salt, and Furs give 💰 Gold; sacred goods such as Incense and Wine give ✨ Faith; medicinal goods such as Spices and Mercury give 🧪 Science; and ornaments such as Dyes, Jade, and Silk give 🎭 Culture.

⬦

Exhausted Foraging Ground

—

A spot just foraged, worked, or hunted out. Nothing more can be taken here until it recovers in time.

⬦

Foraging Camp

🔬 Stone Tools
+1 🌾Food

Unlocks the Tribesperson ability to construct Foraging Camps.

+1 🌾 Food. Must be built on Woods or Rainforest. Once Gathering is researched, can also be built on Marsh and Floodplains. Cannot be built adjacent to another Foraging Camp.

Once Food Drying is researched, provides +1 💰 Gold if adjacent to a Pit House.

⬦

Burial Mound

🎭 Burial Rites
+1 ✨Faith

Unlocks the Tribesperson ability to construct Burial Mounds.

+1 ✨ Faith. Cannot be built on hills or adjacent to another Burial Mound.

⬦

Totem

🎭 Primal Faith
+1 ✨Faith

Unlocks the Tribesperson ability to construct Totems.

+1 ✨ Faith. Provides 🎭 Culture equal to 50% of the tile's Appeal. Can only be built on tiles with Charming or better Appeal, and not adjacent to another Totem.

⬦

Cave Painting

🎭 Primal Art

Unlocks the Tribesperson ability to construct Cave Paintings.

+1 🎭 Culture to adjacent tiles. Can only be built on Mountains. One per city.

⬦

Knapping Site

+1 🔨Production

Unlocks the Tribesperson ability to construct Knapping Sites.

+1 🔨 Production. Must be built on flat land adjacent to a River, or on Floodplains. Once Needle is researched, can also be built on Tundra and Snow. Cannot be built adjacent to another Knapping Site.

⬦

Fishing Camp

🔬 Shore Fishing
+1 🌾Food

Unlocks the Tribesperson ability to construct Fishing Camps.

+1 🌾 Food. Must be built on flat coastal land. Once Needle is researched, can also be built on Tundra and Snow. Cannot be built adjacent to another Fishing Camp.

Once Food Drying is researched, provides +1 💰 Gold if adjacent to a Pit House.

⬦

Pit House

🔬 Sedentism
+1 💰Gold

Unlocks the Tribesperson ability to construct Pit Houses.

+1 💰 Gold and +0.5 🏠 Housing. Must be built on flat land. Once Needle is researched, can also be built on Tundra and Snow.

⬦

Palisade

🔬 Sedentism

Unlocks the Tribesperson and Builder ability to construct Palisades.

Grants +2 ⚔️ Combat Strength to units defending on this tile, which automatically gain 2 turns of fortification. Reduces the Appeal of adjacent tiles by 1. Can only be built on the frontier of your territory.

⬦

Observation Cairn

🔬 Observation of Nature
+1 ✨Faith

Unlocks the Tribesperson ability to construct Observation Cairns.

+1 ✨ Faith. Provides 🧪 Science equal to 50% of the tile's Appeal. Can only be built on Hills with Charming or better Appeal, and not adjacent to another Observation Cairn.

Wandering Start actions

These aren't built the usual way — they're the roaming band's actions (gathering, hunting, raising an Outpost, recruiting) in the Wandering Start game mode.

⬦

Exhausted Foraging Ground

—

A spot just foraged, worked, or hunted out. Nothing more can be taken here until it recovers in time.

⬦

Food Gathering

—

Gather 🌾 Food from this tile equal to its Food yield (at least 1). Can be done on Woods, Rainforest, and edible resources. Once Gathering is researched, can also be done on Marsh, Floodplains, and Oases. The tile is left exhausted and recovers only after a time. The Food is added to your stores, which your bands consume each turn.

⬦

Material Gathering

—

Gather 🔨 Materials from this tile equal to its Production yield. Can be done on Woods, Rainforest, mineral resources, and fiber and spice resources. Once Knapping is researched, can also be done on Hills. The tile is left exhausted and recovers only after a time. The Materials are added to your stores.

⬦

Coastal Fishing

—

Gather 🌾 Food from this tile equal to its Food yield (at least 1). Can be done beside the sea after researching Shore Fishing. The tile is left exhausted and recovers only after a time. The Food is added to your stores, which your bands consume each turn.

⬦

Freshwater Fishing

🔬 Gathering

Gather 🌾 Food from this tile equal to its Food yield (at least 1). Can be done beside a river after researching Gathering. The tile is left exhausted and recovers only after a time. The Food is added to your stores, which your bands consume each turn.

⬦

Open-Water Fishing

—

Gather 🌾 Food from this water tile equal to its Food yield (at least 1). Can be done only in locations such as a sea resource or a reef, and only by an embarked band, after researching Rafts. The tile is left exhausted and recovers only after a time. The Food is added to your stores, which your bands consume each turn.

⬦

Open-Water Hunt

—

Can be done only on sea animals such as Turtles and Whales, and only by an embarked band, after researching Rafts. A successful hunt yields both 🌾 Food and 🔨 Materials and adds them to your stores, but the hunt may fail. Either way the tile is left exhausted and recovers only after a time.

⬦

Hunt

🔬 Hunting

Can be done only on animal resources. A successful hunt yields both 🌾 Food and 🔨 Materials and adds them to your stores, but the hunt may fail. Either way the tile is left exhausted and recovers only after a time.

⬦

Flintknapping

🔬 Stone Tools

Spend 🔨 3 materials for a temporary effect: for a few turns your bands gather more from each 🌾 Food and 🔨 Material Gathering, hunt with a better chance of success, and your bands and recruited units gain ⚔️ Combat Strength. Repeat the action to renew it.

After researching Ground Stone Tools, the effect lasts longer and is stronger.

Costs 🔨 1 less on tiles next to a River, on Hills, or with Stone. Can be done on any dry land, even outside your territory.

⬦

Outpost

🔬 Fire

Build an outpost here for 🔨 10 materials. Each turn it stands, it adds this tile's 🌾 Food and 🔨 Production to your stores, and a unit defending on it gains +2 ⚔️ Combat Strength. If a unit from another civilization, city-state, or the barbarians steps onto it, the outpost is torn down.
