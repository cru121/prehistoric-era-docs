Dedications

A Dedication is a broad gameplay focus your leader chooses for their people each time the world advances into a new World Era. You always have a Dedication in effect — except at the very start, before anyone has had a chance to earn Era Score. Because every game begins in the Prehistoric era, the first Dedication is offered when you advance into the Ancient era.

Each Dedication gives a different reward depending on the Age you enter: a powerful Golden Age bonus if you earned a Golden Age, or an Era-Score quest during a Normal or Dark Age that helps you climb back toward the next Golden Age.

The mod adds 3 Prehistoric-themed Dedications, offered at the Prehistoric → Ancient transition. The base-game Monumentality Dedication is also brought forward to appear in this same window (it fits the dawn of settled civilization — Builders, Settlers and districts); the remaining base-game Dedications resume as normal from the Ancient → Classical transition onward.

🌑 Normal / Dark Age Gain +1 Era Score each time you construct an improvement or building that provides ✨ Faith as a base yield.

Age of Conquest

Dedication
☀️ Golden Age +10 ⚔️ Combat Strength for all your units when attacking a city or district. Gain 💰 Gold equal to the ⚔️ Combat Strength of each unit you kill.
🌑 Normal / Dark Age Gain +1 Era Score each time you kill a non-Barbarian enemy unit in combat, and +2 Era Score each time you capture a city.

Dedication
☀️ Golden Age +2 TradeRoute Trade Route capacity. All your TradeRoute Trade Routes provide +4 💰 Gold.
🌑 Normal / Dark Age Gain +1 Era Score each time you establish a TradeRoute Trade Route or develop a new type of Luxury resource for the first time.
