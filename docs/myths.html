Origin Myths

A Wandering Start feature. When your roaming band founds its first city, the elders tell the story of how your people came to be — and you choose one of 25 origin myths, each granting a permanent bonus. Which myths are offered is shaped by the journey your band actually walked (the "journey" line on each card below is what earns it).

Your first city stands, and the road your band has walked becomes a story. By the fire, the elders tell two tales of how your people came to be. Which shall be the origin of your people?
