Pantheons

14 new pantheon beliefs themed for the stone age. As in the base game, you found a Pantheon with your first accumulated ✨ Faith and choose one belief — these join the base-game pantheons in the pool, giving early, terrain- and ritual-focused options that fit the Prehistoric era.
