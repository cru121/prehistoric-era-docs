Game options

Setup choices the mod adds to the game-creation screen.

Standard game

Options available when setting up any game — no companion mod needed.

Starting Era → Prehistoric Era

Dropdown · Default: Prehistoric Era

The mod adds the Prehistoric Era to the base game's Start Era dropdown and makes it the new default, so a game begins in the Stone Age. Pick a later era to “skip prehistory”: its prehistoric technologies and civics are auto-granted as an earlier era. Turning on Wandering Start forces the start era to Prehistoric Era (the dropdown is locked).

Prehistoric Era Length

Dropdown · Default: Standard

How long the Prehistoric era lasts, by scaling the research cost of its tech and civic trees. Only applies when starting in the Prehistoric era.

Choices
  • Very Short — The briefest prehistoric age (33% shorter) — reach the Ancient era as soon as possible.
  • Short — A shorter prehistoric age (17% shorter) — move on more quickly.
  • Standard — The default, balanced prehistoric pacing.
  • Long — A longer prehistoric age (17% longer) — spend more time before the Ancient era.
  • Very Long — An even longer prehistoric age (33% longer) — the most drawn-out start.

Origin Myths

Toggle · Default: Off · Hidden with Wandering Start

Once you have researched Oral Tradition, your city can complete the Weave the Origin Myth project to choose an Origin Myth. The effect of the chosen myth lasts for the rest of the game.

Surplus Production Projects

Toggle · Default: Off

Adds two repeatable city projects, The Daylight Circle and The Firelight Circle, available in a city with a Hearth. Each turns part of the Production put into it into Science or Culture. Useful when research is far slower than production. The default is disabled.

Wandering Start Mode

Toggle · Default: Off

This game mode begins the game before the dawn of cities. Instead of a Settler, each civilization starts as a roaming Wandering Band that cannot found a city, and you must endure the Stone Age as hunter-gatherers before putting down roots.

Your bands live off the land. Forage wild food and raw materials from the terrain around you, and hunt game once you have learned Hunting. Each band must eat every turn: a fed band recovers, while a starving one weakens and may perish. Bands grow more capable as they forage and hunt, earning promotions along the way.

Gather enough materials to light a campfire and raise an outpost, and recruit specialists to strengthen your band. Even before you found a city, your people press on, gaining Science and Culture every turn.

Research Sedentism to settle your first city and rejoin the familiar course of history. Lose all your units before you settle, and your people vanish from the world.

Wandering Start sub-options

These appear only when the Wandering Start Mode game mode is enabled, fine-tuning how the roaming start plays out.

City-State Outposts

Toggle · Default: On · Shown only with Wandering Start

If enabled, city-states begin the game already settled beside an outpost. If disabled, city-states also start as wandering bands: they roam their homeland, make camp midway through the age, and still found their city on schedule. The default is enabled.

AI Outpost Start

Toggle · Default: Off · Shown only with Wandering Start

If enabled, AI civilizations begin the game already settled beside an outpost like city-states, skipping the wandering stage; they found their city upon researching Sedentism, or at the latest when a grace period ends. If disabled, AI civilizations wander and forage before making camp on their own. The default is disabled.

Longer Wandering

Toggle · Default: Off · Shown only with Wandering Start

If enabled, the technology tree is reshuffled so that Sedentism moves later: you must research other prehistoric technologies first before you can research Sedentism and settle down. The default is disabled.

Compatibility options

These appear only when the matching companion Workshop mod is also enabled.

All Prehistoric Wildlife

Toggle · Default: Off

When the Prehistoric Wildlife mod is also in use, this mod by default keeps dinosaurs and other creatures that never lived alongside humans from appearing. If enabled, that restriction is lifted and every species, dinosaurs included, can appear. The default is disabled.

Only appears when the Prehistoric Wildlife mod is also enabled.

Timeline

Dropdown · Hidden with Wandering Start

Which set of spawn dates Historical Spawn Dates uses. The Prehistoric Era mod adds a fifth option of its own; the other four are the ones that mod ships with.

Only appears when the Historical Spawn Dates mod is also enabled.

Choices
  • Prehistoric — Each civilization appears when people first settled its land year-round, long before any state was founded. Dates are earlier than Standard throughout, for those who want to live through the prehistoric cultures themselves. Added by the Prehistoric Era mod.
