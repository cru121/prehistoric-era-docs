Prehistoric Era

A new starting era for Civilization VI — begin in the Stone Age, before the Ancient era.

Stone is chipped to an edge, fire is coaxed from the dark, and your people roam in small bands after the hunt. No settled land is yet theirs, no city raised stone upon stone. Yet within that first trembling flame, within that first sharpened blade, lies the seed of every civilization to come. From this raw dawn, you will lead humankind to take its first steps on the long road toward the stars.
