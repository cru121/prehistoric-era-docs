Governments

3 Prehistoric Tier-0 governments, all unlocked together when you research your first civic, Band Society. Sharing Community is the era's starting government; from there you may switch between the three.

ℹ️ To keep a government's Legacy bonus after switching, build its Tier-0 Government Plaza building (Council House, Muster Square, Tribute Warehouse) in the Government District (unlocked by the base-game State Workforce civic).

Sharing Community

Tier 0 Government (the era's starting government)
💰 2× Economic
Unlocked by Researching Band Society (your first civic)
Bonus +0.2 🌾 Food per 👤 Citizen in all cities.
Legacy bonus +5% 🎭 Culture.
🏛️

Big-Man Society

Tier 0 Government
⚔️ 1× Military 💰 1× Economic
Unlocked by Researching Band Society (your first civic)
Bonus +2 💰 Gold and +2 Loyalty per turn for cities with a garrisoned unit.
Legacy bonus +15% Production toward City Center buildings.

Warrior Confederacy

Tier 0 Government
⚔️ 2× Military
Unlocked by Researching Band Society (your first civic)
Bonus +3 ⚔️ Combat Strength when attacking. Increases Healing by +3.
Legacy bonus Yields gained from pillaging improvements are increased by 50%.
